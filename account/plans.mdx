TinyTrack is priced by monthly events — the number of pageviews and custom events your sites record each month. Whether you run a personal project or a growing product, both plans start with a **14-day free trial** so you can track your first visitors before spending a cent.

## Plan Comparison

Choose the plan that fits your stage. Both plans include 10k monthly events, a real-time dashboard, goals and funnels, and shareable dashboards.

## Free Trial

Start tracking immediately without entering a credit card. Every new account gets **14 days of full Pro access** — including API access, Search Console integration, and X Mentions — so you can evaluate every feature before you decide.

Your trial begins the moment you create your account. At the end of the 14 days, choose a plan to continue. If you don't subscribe, your dashboard becomes read-only and no new events are recorded.

## Annual Billing

Pay for a full year upfront and get **2 months free** — the equivalent of paying for 10 months instead of 12. Annual billing is available for both Starter and Pro.

Switch to annual billing at any time from the **Settings → Billing** page. The change takes effect immediately and TinyTrack prorates any remaining monthly credit toward your annual charge.

## Choosing a Plan

**Start with Starter** if you want straightforward pageview and event tracking with a real-time dashboard, goals, funnels, and shareable links. At $9/mo it covers the core analytics workflow for most small sites and indie products.

**Upgrade to Pro** when you need any of the following:

- **API access** — pull your stats programmatically into your own dashboards, scripts, or data pipelines
- **Search Console integration** — see which Google search queries are driving traffic, directly inside TinyTrack
- **X Mentions** — monitor when your site or product is mentioned on X (formerly Twitter)
- **Priority support** — get faster responses from the TinyTrack team

<Tip>
  You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from the **Billing** settings page.
</Tip>
