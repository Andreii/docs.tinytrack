## How it works

Getting up and running with TinyTrack takes under five minutes and requires no coding knowledge.

1. **Register at [tinytrack.io](http://tinytrack.io)** and register your domain.
2. **Install the script** by pasting the `<script>` tag into the `<head>` of your website. 
3. **Observe the traffic** in the dashboard! You can explore live visitor counts, historical trends, top pages, traffic sources, goal conversion and funnel performance.
