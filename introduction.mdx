TinyTrack is a cookie-free web analytics platform built for founders, developers, and marketing teams who want accurate, real-time data without compromising visitor privacy. Drop a single 1 KB script into your site's `<head>`, and within seconds you'll see live pageviews, unique visitor counts, goal conversions, and funnel performance — all without setting a single cookie or displaying a consent banner. TinyTrack gives you the numbers that move your product while keeping you fully compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and PECR.

## How it works

Getting up and running with TinyTrack takes under five minutes and requires no backend changes.

**1. Install the script.** Paste a single `<script>` tag into the `<head>` of your website. The tag is asynchronous and weighs just 1 KB, so it won't slow your page or affect your Lighthouse score.

**2. Data flows in automatically.** The moment visitors land on your site, TinyTrack begins counting pageviews, recording referral sources, and filtering out bot traffic. Every event reaches your dashboard in real time — no configuration required to start seeing data.

**3. Open your dashboard and act.** Log in to your TinyTrack dashboard to explore live visitor counts, historical trends, top pages, traffic sources, goal conversions, and funnel performance — all on one screen.
