# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This policy explains how Tinytrack handles information when you use our website, create an account, or use our analytics service.

## Information we process

We process account details such as your name and email address, billing and subscription details supplied through our payment provider, and the website and analytics data you choose to send to Tinytrack. We may also process limited technical information needed to secure and operate the service.

## How we use information

We use information to provide the service, maintain accounts, process subscriptions, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, and improve Tinytrack. We do not sell personal information.

## Analytics data

Tinytrack is designed for privacy-first website analytics. The service does not set tracking cookies for its analytics collection. Website owners are responsible for configuring their sites and using the service in accordance with the privacy laws that apply to them.

## Sharing and service providers

We share information only with service providers that help us operate Tinytrack, such as hosting, authentication, and payment providers, or when required by law. These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services.

## Retention and security

We keep information for as long as needed to provide Tinytrack, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices

You can access, update, or delete account information through the service where available. To request help with your information or exercise applicable privacy rights, contact us at [act@omtia.com](mailto:act@omtia.com).

## Updates to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
